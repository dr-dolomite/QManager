## Risks / rollback

- A broken tarball release: delete the release + tag, fix, re-release (OTA users only pick it up on check).
- A broken feed package after merge: bump to `0.1.36` with the fix. A re-release of `0.1.35` with changed content gets CI's auto-bump to `0.1.35-1`, which works but desyncs the version.
- The feed PR is outward-facing to iamromulan; nothing reaches devices until both of his merges.
